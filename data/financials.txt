# Financial Summary — Sunrise Coffee Roasters LLC (FY 2025)

Revenue for FY 2025 was INR 2.4 crore, up 18% year over year. Gross margin is 41%.
Operating cash flow is INR 28 lakh, and the company carries existing debt of
INR 9 lakh against equipment purchased in 2023.

The requested loan of INR 40,00,000 would be secured against the new Bengaluru
facility. Projected debt-service coverage ratio under the loan is 1.9x.

Monthly payroll runs to INR 11 lakh. The company has maintained a single bank
account with South Bank since incorporation and has never missed a supplier
payment, per trade references from GreenLeaf Plantations and Hilltop Estates.
