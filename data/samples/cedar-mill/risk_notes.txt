# Risk Notes — Cedar Mill Furniture Pvt Ltd

Underwriter notes from the first-pass review:

- Revenue concentration: Northline Home Stores alone contributes 41% of revenue.
  Loss of this buyer would cut the debt-service coverage ratio below 1.1x.
- External repayment record exists: ICICI term loan since 2022, no arrears.
  Credit history is not thin.
- The founders, Rohan Mehta and Kavita Iyer, have no prior defaults and
  carry personal credit scores above 765.
- Aravalli Timber reports that Cedar Mill Furniture has paid on time for
  every delivery since 2020.
- Policy 7.3 does not trigger: HighRiskFlag is present, ThinCreditHistory is not.
