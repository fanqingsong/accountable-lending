# Financial Summary — Cedar Mill Furniture Pvt Ltd (FY 2025)

Revenue for FY 2025 was INR 3.8 crore, up 11% year over year. Gross margin is 33%.
Operating cash flow is INR 21 lakh. Existing ICICI term loan outstanding is
INR 14 lakh against machinery purchased in 2022.

The requested loan of INR 35,00,000 would be secured against the new CNC line.
Projected debt-service coverage ratio under the loan is 1.6x.

Monthly payroll runs to INR 9 lakh. The company has maintained a single bank
account with ICICI Bank since incorporation. Trade references from Aravalli Timber
and Desert Oak Suppliers report no missed payments since 2020.
