# Business Profile — Cedar Mill Furniture Pvt Ltd

Cedar Mill Furniture Pvt Ltd is a hardwood furniture workshop based in Jaipur, Rajasthan.
Founded in 2019 by Rohan Mehta and Kavita Iyer, the company employs 19 people and
sells dining sets and custom cabinetry to 22 retailers in North India.

The company's largest buyer is Northline Home Stores, a regional chain of 11 outlets,
responsible for 41% of sales. Cedar Mill already holds a term loan with ICICI Bank,
in place since 2022, and has never missed a scheduled repayment.

The founders are seeking a capex loan of INR 35,00,000 to install a CNC line
at the Jaipur workshop.
