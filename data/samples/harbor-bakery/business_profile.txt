# Business Profile — Harbor Bakehouse Pvt Ltd

Harbor Bakehouse Pvt Ltd is a wholesale bakery based in Pune, Maharashtra.
Founded in 2016 by Priya Deshmukh, the company employs 28 people and supplies
bread and pastries to 60 hotels across western India.

No single customer accounts for more than 22% of revenue. The company already
holds a working-capital facility with HDFC Bank, in place since 2021, and has
never missed a scheduled repayment.

The founders are seeking an additional working-capital loan of INR 25,00,000
to add a second production line in Pune.
