# Business Profile — Sunrise Coffee Roasters LLC

Sunrise Coffee Roasters LLC is a specialty coffee roasting company based in Kochi, Kerala.
Founded in 2018 by Maya Krishnan and Anil Thomas, the company employs 12 people and
sells wholesale beans to 45 cafés across South India.

The company's flagship product is the Monsoon Malabar blend, which accounts for 60%
of revenue. Sunrise Coffee Roasters holds long-term supply contracts with GreenLeaf
Plantations and Hilltop Estates. Their largest single customer is Café Amara, a
chain of 15 outlets, responsible for 38% of sales.

The founders are seeking a working-capital loan of INR 40,00,000 to open a second
roasting facility in Bengaluru. The company has no prior borrowing history with any
scheduled commercial bank.
