# Risk Notes — Sunrise Coffee Roasters LLC

Underwriter notes from the first-pass review:

- Revenue concentration: Café Amara alone contributes 38% of revenue. Loss of
  this customer would cut the debt-service coverage ratio below 1.0x.
- Thin credit history: the company has never borrowed from a scheduled
  commercial bank, so there is no external repayment record.
- The founders, Maya Krishnan and Anil Thomas, have no prior defaults and
  carry personal credit scores above 780.
- GreenLeaf Plantations reports that Sunrise Coffee Roasters has paid on time
  for every delivery since 2019.
